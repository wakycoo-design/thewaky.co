01 / MINDLAP THERAPY

Turning a fragmented therapy workflow into one connected system.

Designing the operational architecture behind booking, payments, packages, customer records and session communication.

THE CHALLENGE

Many moving parts. One customer journey.

Therapy operations involve more than simply scheduling an appointment. Customers may need to verify their identity, select a session, make a payment, use an existing package, receive confirmations and eventually access their session.

Behind that customer journey sits another layer: customer records, package credits, payments, invoices, session information and administrative visibility.

The challenge was to make these pieces work together without turning the operation into a collection of disconnected manual processes.

System architecture

Waky designed the workflow around specialised tools, allowing each platform to do the job it is best suited for while connecting the overall business logic.

01 Website — customer entry point
02 WhatsApp OTP — customer verification
03 Zoho Creator — business logic and orchestration
04 Zoho Bookings — availability and appointment scheduling
05 Payment & package logic — handling booking conditions
06 CRM + Zoho Books — customer and financial visibility
07 WhatsApp automation — confirmations, reminders and alerts
THE APPROACH

Don't rebuild the wheel. Connect the right pieces.

One of the key architectural decisions was to keep appointment scheduling inside Zoho Bookings rather than attempting to recreate a scheduling engine in another system.

Zoho Creator acts around the scheduling layer, handling business rules, package logic, customer information and the connections between systems.

This creates a clearer separation between availability, business logic, financial workflows and communication.

Booking workflow

A structured booking journey built around real-time scheduling and availability rather than duplicating the scheduling engine.

Payment & packages

Business logic distinguishes between customers using existing package credits and customers requiring a new payment flow.

CRM & financial visibility

Customer, session, package and revenue information can be connected across CRM and Zoho Books for administrative visibility.

Automated communication

Key moments in the customer journey can trigger WhatsApp communication, including confirmations, reminders, cancellations and operational alerts.

THE RESULT

Instead of treating booking, payments, CRM and communication as separate processes, the workflow is designed as one connected operational system.

02 / TORCHE WATERPROOFING

Bringing sales and business operations into one system.

A business-system approach for a construction waterproofing company managing leads, customers, operations and growth.

THE CHALLENGE

Growth creates more moving pieces.

As a service business grows, information starts moving between enquiries, sales conversations, customers, projects, purchasing and operations.

Without a connected structure, important information can become scattered across people, spreadsheets, messages and individual tools.

The goal was to create a clearer operational layer that could support sales and business execution as the company grows.

Business system

The focus is not simply on adding another software tool. It is on designing how information should move through the business.

01 Lead generation & enquiry
02 Sales qualification
03 Customer & project information
04 Operational coordination
05 Follow-up & business visibility
THE RESULT

A clearer foundation for managing sales and business operations as the company moves from individual processes toward a connected system.
